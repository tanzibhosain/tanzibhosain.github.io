Md Tanzib Hosain

I am a recent research intern at MPI-SP, where I'm working on finding socially inclusive and personalized behavioral patterns of LLMs.

My research centers on AI for mathematical and scientific understanding, spanning (1) Language Models—LLMs as search agents, multimodal pre-training, generative models for mathematics, LLMs for code generation and scientific discovery, and reinforcing language agents for reasoning—and (2) AI4Science, including transformer and graph neural network approaches to learning algorithms, machine learning, human-computer interaction, and optimization for scientific discovery.

I collaborate with Prof. Mohammad Ali Moni (UQ) on artificial intelligence in medicine and retrieval augmented generation; Prof. Usman Naseem (MQ) on machine translation and linguistic fairness or bias; and Md Rizwan Parvez, PhD (QCRI) on agents and reasoning.
